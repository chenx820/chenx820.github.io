# Hardware explorer photographs

The active routes use real equipment photographs and experimental atom imaging.
The photonics view focuses on the hardware photograph in a published figure using
a CSS viewport; the original embedded raster is kept intact. Other images are
displayed proportionally without cropping. No images are AI-generated.
The former SVG illustrations are no longer referenced by the explorer.

## Neutral atoms

- Asset: `cesium-optical-tweezer-array.png` (643 × 644).
- Author: Preston Huft (PrestonHuft); author's own experimental image.
- Depicts: fluorescence from cesium-133 atoms in 1225 laser-generated optical
  traps, arranged with 3 micrometre spacing.
- This is a false-color average of several experimental images, with background
  normalization and noise suppression. It is not a single-shot fully occupied
  array, nor an image of a quantum state. The UI explicitly identifies the
  averaging and false-color treatment.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1225_site_cold_cesium_atom_array.png
- Download: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5e/1225_site_cold_cesium_atom_array.png
- License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Photonics

- Asset: `quix-photonic-processor-figure.jpg` (1166 × 773).
- Extracted intact from page 2, embedded image Im3.jpg, of the paper below.
- The CSS viewport shows panel 1(c), approximately x=81, y=257, w=400, h=248,
  preserving its original labels. No raster retouching or generated details.
- Depicts: assembled QuiX 12-mode photonic processor, fiber arrays, control
  electronics, and thermal-control components. The waveguides themselves are
  not resolved in this photograph; propagation and interference are described
  in the accompanying teaching text. Twelve modes does not mean twelve qubits.
- Source: C. Taballione et al., “A universal fully reconfigurable 12-mode quantum
  photonic processor”, Materials for Quantum Technology 1 (2021) 035002, Fig. 1(c).
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/2633-4356/ac168c
- Open-access PDF: https://ris.utwente.nl/ws/files/264552326/Taballione_2021_Mater._Quantum_Technol._1_035002.pdf
- License: CC BY 4.0, explicitly stated on page 1:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
